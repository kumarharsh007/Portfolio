Chapter 01 / The analyst

Curious about the player behind the numbers.

As an Associate Product Analyst at Electronic Arts, I partner with Product Managers to deliver data-driven insights that inform strategic decisions. My work spans Real Racing 3, NBA Live Mobile, Madden Mobile, and College Football Mobile, where I help teams understand feature performance and player behavior through comprehensive analysis.

I specialize in A/B testing, feature performance analysis, and telemetry design—translating complex gaming data into actionable insights. I work primarily with SQL, Tableau, and Python, and use AI-augmented workflows with Claude Code, Gemini CLI, and Snowflake Cortex to optimize queries, automate repetitive tasks, and accelerate analysis. I’m also expanding my toolkit in machine learning, statistics, and cloud-based ETL pipelines with Airflow.
