01Player behavior · Segmentation

Behavioral segmentation

Building a more complete picture of how players engage with modes, progression, and the game over time.

SQLPythonClusteringFeature engineering

The question

How can product teams move beyond a single engagement or spending tier to understand different patterns of play?

The approach

Combined gameplay, progression, content preference, and monetization signals into player-level features. Compared clustering solutions, profiled resulting groups using interpretable measures, and considered how cohort and observation-window choices affected the analysis.

What it supported

The work produced a behavioral framework and player profiles for Product, Live Ops, and Design discussions—not a production personalization system or a proven intervention.

Context: The analysis was observational. Seasonal concentration and saved revenue-definition questions limit generalization and require validation before operational use.

02Early behavior · Monetization

Early-player signals

Examining which early gameplay and progression patterns were associated with later payer conversion.

SQLPythonCohort analysisStatistical testing
Read the case study +

The question

Which early player behaviors were associated with eventual payer conversion, and could those signals be separated from behavior that followed a purchase?

The approach

Reviewed gameplay, card-acquisition, event, and currency telemetry; screened interpretable signals; and revisited the analysis with purchase-timing checks after stakeholder feedback raised circularity concerns.

What it supported

The analysis produced a compact set of candidate behavioral signals and an interpretable framework for further evaluation. It sharpened the distinction between early activity that preceded observed purchases and activity that may have followed them.

Context: These are associations, not causes or a deployed prediction model. The observed purchase window and validation design do not establish future performance or incremental conversion.

03Progression · Live operations

Program completion

Understanding how player activity and purchase patterns looked around progression milestones.

SQLWithin-player analysisLive operationsCommunication
Read the case study +

The question

How do spending and engagement patterns change around program completion, and what might continued progression activity suggest for future milestone design?

The approach

Compared eligible players with themselves before and after completion, aligned spend classifications to the pre-completion period, and made timing and eligibility rules explicit in the reporting.

What it supported

Prepared a stakeholder-facing analysis and a recommendation to test progression and milestone pacing. The comparison helped frame a product question for further testing.

Context: The findings are descriptive, not causal. Completion-based selection and calendar timing remain possible explanations; the recommendation is not evidence that a change shipped or improved outcomes.

04AI enablement · Self-service analytics

Game data, ready for AI

Giving AI and PMs game-specific context so questions start from documented data definitions instead of guesswork.

GitHub CopilotClaudeGemini EnterpriseSnowflake CoCo
Read the workflow +

The challenge

Game data structures are specific: table names, column meanings, grains, and metric definitions can vary. Without that context, an AI assistant may make unsupported assumptions.

The approach

Created game-specific skill files and Markdown references that explain relevant tables, columns, and data definitions. Applied the same context-first approach in Snowflake CoCo and shared the references with PMs.

How it helps

PMs can ask more informed questions and get a quicker first response grounded in documented game context, while I can focus more time on deeper analysis. The documentation gives the assistant context; it does not train the underlying model or guarantee that every answer is correct.

Guardrail: Treat AI responses as a starting point. Validate query logic, metric definitions, and results against authoritative sources before using them to make decisions. No time-saved or accuracy lift is claimed.
