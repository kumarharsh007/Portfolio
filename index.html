Chapter 01 / The analyst

Curious about the player behind the numbers.

As an Associate Product Analyst at Electronic Arts, I partner with Product Managers to deliver data-driven insights that inform strategic decisions. My work spans mobile titles including Real Racing 3, NBA Live Mobile, and Madden Mobile, where I help teams understand feature performance and player behavior through comprehensive analysis.

I specialize in A/B testing, feature performance analysis, and telemetry design—translating complex gaming data into actionable insights. Working primarily with SQL, Tableau, and Python, I’m expanding my toolkit into machine learning, statistics, and cloud-based ETL pipelines and Airflow. As an avid gamer with a deep appreciation for AAA titles, I bring both analytical rigor and genuine passion for understanding what makes gaming experiences truly engaging.
